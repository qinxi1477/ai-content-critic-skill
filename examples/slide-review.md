# Slide Review Example

Input: One-slide AI-generated pitch deck page with a title, four cards, icons, and a chart.

Output:

## What Fails At Thumbnail Size

- `Major` The four cards have equal visual weight, so the slide has no obvious takeaway.
- `Major` The chart is too small to function as evidence; it looks like decoration.

## What Fails At Reading Size

- `Major` The title says "Transforming Operations With AI", but none of the cards names an operational workflow.
- `Minor` Icon styles are inconsistent: two are outlined, one is filled, one uses a different stroke weight.

## Highest-Impact Layout Changes

- Replace the four-card grid with one main claim and one proof chart.
- Enlarge the chart and label the single metric the audience should remember.
- Cut each card to a 3-5 word label if the grid must stay.

## Copy To Cut Or Rewrite

- Cut "unlocking seamless innovation".
- Rewrite "AI-powered optimization" into the specific process being optimized.
