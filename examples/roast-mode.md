# Roast Mode Example

Input: "Roast this AI-generated landing page hero."

Output:

## Verdict

This hero looks like it was assembled from a startup pitch deck, a meditation app, and a vocabulary list titled "Words That Sound Fundable."

## Biggest Problems

- `Major` Headline — "Empower Your Future With Intelligent Solutions" says nothing. It could sell accounting software, a toothbrush, or a suspiciously confident kitchen appliance.
- `Major` Visual — the glowing abstract orb is doing the job of a product screenshot because there apparently is no product brave enough to appear.
- `Major` CTA — "Get Started" has no context. Started doing what? Paying? Meditating? Apologizing?

## Fixes

- Make the headline name the product category or literal offer.
- Replace the abstract visual with the actual interface, result, or workflow.
- Rewrite the CTA around the next real action, such as "Upload a CSV" or "Generate a draft report".
