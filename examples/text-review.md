# Text Review Example

Input:

> In today's fast-paced digital landscape, innovation is more important than ever. Companies must leverage cutting-edge technology to unlock new opportunities and drive meaningful impact.

Output:

## Verdict

This reads like generic AI business filler: polished on the surface, but almost no specific claim survives contact with oxygen.

## Biggest Problems

- `Major` Empty opening — "fast-paced digital landscape" and "more important than ever" are stock phrases that do not say what changed, for whom, or why now.
- `Major` Fake specificity — "cutting-edge technology" sounds concrete but names no tool, workflow, market, or measurable outcome.
- `Minor` Recycled cadence — the sentence pair follows a familiar AI rhythm: broad context, vague imperative, abstract benefit.

## Fixes

- Replace the universal claim with a specific pressure, audience, or decision.
- Name the technology or behavior being discussed.
- Replace "meaningful impact" with the actual business or human outcome.
